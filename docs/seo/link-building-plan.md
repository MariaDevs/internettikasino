## 2. Ground rules

1. **Real sites only.** Every link source must have organic traffic (Ahrefs traffic > 200/mo), Finnish or Nordic audience, and content beyond link posts.
2. **Red flags — skip the site:**
   - High DR with near-zero traffic (e.g. DR 65, 250 visits) — almost always a link network
   - "Write for us" pages listing casino, crypto, CBD and pharma side by side
   - Hundreds of outbound casino links per month
   - Anything sold through Telegram, Fiverr or "DA 50 PBN" marketplaces — this is exactly the spam already pointing at the domain
3. **Clean anchor profile.** The domain already carries ~260 spam domains with junk anchors. New links should look natural (see §5).
4. **Paid placements and Google's rules.** Google's guidelines say paid links should carry `rel="sponsored"`. Paid dofollow guest posts are industry practice in iGaming affiliate SEO, but they are a policy risk that grows with volume. Keep paid placements to a minority, on genuine sites, and earn the rest (§4 angles 1–3 are built to be earned).
5. **Log everything** in a sheet: date, URL, DR, traffic, anchor, target page, cost, rel attribute, live-check date.

## 4. Pitch angles

1. **"Milloin nettikasinon voitto on verovapaa?"** — a reader-service explainer: winnings from games run in an EEA state are tax-free, others taxable, and Brexit moved Gibraltar, Isle of Man and UK licences to the taxable side. Note: the competitors ranking for "verovapaat kasinot" already state this correctly (checked 2026-11-01), so pitch it as a clear guide, **not** as "other sites get it wrong". Pitch to: helsinkitimes.fi (in English), financist.fi, laskin.fi, pelituutiset.fi, uutisankka.com, europeangaming.eu. Drafts: `docs/seo/pitches/tax-explainer-pitches.md`. Link: `/verovapaat-kasinot/`.
2. **Rahapelilaki 2027 explainer** — what changes for players when the licence market opens. Timely all through 2026–27. Pitch to sports and news sites. Link: `/suomen-rahapelilaki-2027/`.
3. **"Kuinka nopeasti nettikasino maksaa?"** — a small data piece from our withdrawal-time tests across 22 casinos. Pitch to tech and finance sites (mobiili.fi, nopeustesti.fi, financist.fi). Link: `/nopein-kasino/` or `/pikakasinot/`.
4. **Game rules** — blackjack basic strategy, roulette odds. Pitch to card and game sites as a guest guide. Links: `/blackjack-saannot/`, `/ruletti/`.
5. **Vastuullinen pelaaminen** — a responsible-gambling resource; some lifestyle sites accept these where they'd refuse a casino ad. Link: `/vastuullinen-pelaaminen/` plus the homepage.

| Anchor type | Share | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand / URL | 50 % | Internettikasino, internettikasino.com |
| Partial / natural | 30 % | "vertailu verovapaista kasinoista", "tämä pikakasino-opas" |
| Generic | 10 % | "lue lisää", "tästä" |
| Exact match | ≤ 10 % | "verovapaat kasinot", "pikakasino" |

Never repeat the same exact-match anchor twice in a month.

## 6. Pace and budget

| Period | Links/month | Mix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Month 1 (Nov) | 4–6 | GPWA, 2 Tier B guest guides, 1 Tier A sponsored, Isle of Man pitch |
| Months 2–3 | 6–8 | Tier A/B mix, follow-ups on earned pitches |
| Months 4–6 | 8–10 | Scale what worked, add Tier C |

A steady pace looks natural; a burst after relaunch on a domain with a spam history does not. Paid Tier A placements in Finland typically cost a few hundred euros each. Get quotes and set the monthly budget before outreach.

## 7. Own network

Link from other Traffic Puma sites only where it's genuinely relevant to a Finnish reader (for example kasinotilmanrekisteroitymista.com). Avoid sitewide footer links and linking every property to every other one — that's an easy footprint for Google to discount. onlinecasinoperu.com already links once; it's Spanish-language, so don't add more.
